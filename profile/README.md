## What it is

On-chain proposals, autonomous AI payments, and instant 70/30 profit sharing on Stellar.

## Why Stellar

Stellar gives us fast, low-cost settlement and a public ledger; **Soroban** lets the rules live on-chain instead of in a database.

## Capabilities

- **Proposals anchored on-chain** · generated as PDFs and committed by SHA-256 hash
- **Magic-link acceptance** · clients review and accept without a wallet onboarding funnel
- **Automatic 70/30 split** · settlement triggers the PaymentSplitter contract, paying collaborators instantly
